# Copied-save startup timing controls

These private controls use repository-local copies of ten actual Disco Elysium save archives, totaling 10,717,188 bytes. The copies were verified against a local manifest by filename hash, content hash, byte length and UTC modification time before and after each measured run. The original SaveGames tree was read only for the initial copy and was not rechecked afterward. No save names, contents or gameplay observations are retained in this report. Both copied-save runs use a `FakeProvider` and an in-memory `SessionHost.attach`, with fixture preparation and test process launch outside their clocks. Neither measures the real CLI prompt, provider initialization, transcript behavior or a player-facing 30-second result.

The [current-path copied-save result](../../.tmp/startup-30/real-full-fresh-result.json) measures 72.568 seconds from immediately before the measured `openBackend` call to in-memory attachment. `openBackend` completed at 24.239 seconds, the fake provider bound at 71.355 seconds and attachment followed verified metadata stop at 72.568 seconds. The fixture asserted runtime ownership, fresh state, ready safety, healthy journal, running watcher, one unacknowledged event, ready fake provider and attachment. Its selected source hash is `409717054477f9b0803abd0c4f24cb0559165a0643634942666d635c46b573d8`. The later private fake turn timed out before acknowledgement, so this artifact proves no acknowledged restart duration.

The separate [optimized copied-save result](../../.tmp/w/.tmp/startup-real-optimized-0aa39be863386990-result.json) measures 48.338 seconds from immediately before its `openBackend` call to in-memory attachment. It uses scratch-only pre-marker metadata opening, warm publication and codec, warm core-reply validation and overlapping worker starts. Its `openBackend` completed at 18.919 seconds, the fake provider was ready at 43.802 seconds, the workers stopped at 45.931 seconds and attachment followed explicit extra fixture job rechecks at 48.338 seconds. Those rechecks occupied 2.406 seconds after the workers had already stopped and verified their own jobs; this is test overhead, not a measured production saving. The result asserts owner, fresh state, ready safety, healthy journal, running watcher, one settled private outbox entry, one unacknowledged journal event, ready fake provider, attachment and verified worker shutdown. Its selected source hash is `71f5dd4a14c9490fc0ed16a26dfd087a27b471e37c9f2c775eab8af143fa578b`. The source lists and fixture implementations differ from the current-path result, so their observed 24.230-second difference is not an isolated causal saving. The scratch workers lack production source attestation and injected failure coverage.

An unchanged [repeat of the optimized copied-save fixture](../../.tmp/w/.tmp/startup-real-optimized-9f80657db1cfd6c5-result.json) reached the same asserted healthy fake-client attachment in 46.459 seconds. Its selected source hash, staged-source hash and save-copy manifest hash match the earlier optimized run, and its post-run checks again passed. Backend opening ended at 17.547 seconds, acquisition and safety reconciliation at 38.029 seconds, and retention, watcher startup, fake-provider binding, verified worker stops, extra fixture checks and attachment filled the remaining 8.430 seconds. The two runs do not establish a production saving or a live 30-second result; even the faster private clock remains 16.459 seconds above the target before process launch, real-provider cost and real-client cost.

A [second current-path copied-save run](../../.tmp/startup-30/real-full-966cdd3be2fe1542-fresh.json) reached the same asserted healthy in-memory attachment in 77.195 seconds, again starting its clock immediately before `openBackend` rather than at process start. Its [bounded interaction diagnostic](../../.tmp/startup-30/real-full-966cdd3be2fe1542-diagnostic.json) shows the later fake turn reached the end of acquisition at 72.348 seconds and preview at 107.078 seconds, then failed at the existing 120-second gate while context preparation was still running. Preparation failed at 138.788 seconds; no fake-provider send or acknowledgement callback ran. The private child stopped, and copied saves, selected repository sources and 91 staged source files passed post-run checks. This run also proves no acknowledged restart duration and makes no live readiness claim.

The separate [synthetic scheduling pair](../../.tmp/w/.tmp/startup-spikes/backend/same-source-scheduling-comparison.json) used a stub save and 62 matching selected source hashes checked before and after each run. Serial worker opening reached its fake-provider and verified-stop boundary in 40.858 seconds; concurrent opening reached the same synthetic boundary in 38.089 seconds, an observed 2.770-second difference. Both workers were ready before first use. That fixture did not attach a client, use a real provider or process the copied saves. One sequential pair cannot establish a repeatable scheduling saving, and its result is not additive with either copied-save run.

The current-path 72.568-second and optimized 48.338-second intervals both exceed the healthy 30-second routine target even before real CLI and provider costs. These controls bound and locate work for the [integrated execution candidate](../features/runtime-startup-execution-30.md); they do not authorize any source, process-stop, publication or readiness change. A private acknowledged-binding restart with the real adapter remains unverified.
